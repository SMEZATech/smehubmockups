About this webinar

In this practical 75-minute live webinar, we explore how South African SME founders can build accurate revenue, staffing, and working capital forecasts with limited historical data.

Whether you're preparing for your next funding round, planning headcount for Q4, or managing inventory cash lock-up, learn how to turn light data into actionable growth predictions using bottom-up unit economics.

What you'll learn

  • How to build a revenue forecast on light data — bottom-up unit economics vs top-down TAM slicing, and when to use which.
  • How to tie your staffing plan to revenue — trigger points for the next 3 hires and how to test them before you commit.
  • Inventory and working capital — reorder points, safety stock and the cash lock-up you can control.
  • The three forecast mistakes founders repeatedly make, and one thing every forecast should include but usually doesn't.
