About this webinar

Most SA SME founders skip forecasting because "we don't have enough data." Then they hire the wrong number of people, sit on the wrong inventory, or run out of cash 4 weeks earlier than expected.

In 75 minutes we'll walk through the exact 3-model stack our advisors use with founders in the first 24 months of operation — a top-line revenue forecast, a staffing forecast tied to revenue, and an inventory / working-capital forecast tied to both.

You'll leave with: a spreadsheet template, a checklist of what data to start capturing today, and a recorded run-through you can share with your finance lead.

What you'll learn

Revenue forecast on light data

Bottom-up unit economics vs top-down TAM slicing — when to use which.

Staffing tied to revenue

Trigger points for the next 3 hires and how to test them before you commit.

Inventory & working capital

Reorder points, safety stock and cash lock-up you can control.

The three forecast mistakes

Common misreads — and one thing every forecast should include but usually doesn't.
