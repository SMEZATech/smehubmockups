The Founder's Edition T-Shirt is designed for the SA business owner who moves between pitch meetings, warehouse walk-throughs and school pickup — often in the same afternoon. Built from a heavier 220gsm combed cotton with a light stretch, it holds its shape after a full day of wear.

Every shirt is finished in a small Cape Town workshop and pre-shrunk so what you get is what stays. The chest crest is embroidered — not printed — so it survives the wash cycle you'll actually put it through.

  • Ultra-soft, breathable 100% combed cotton (220gsm).
  • Tailored regular fit — not boxy, not skinny.
  • Embroidered SME crest at the chest, subtle in white-on-white.
  • Pre-shrunk and colour-locked for real-world laundry days.
