About the Gala

The Funding Gala is the single evening every year where SA's angel investors, VC partners, DFI teams and grant officers land in one room — with 180 founders they've been asked to meet.

Expect four hours of tight programming: a 40-minute state-of-funding keynote, three moderated founder pitches, an on-stage LP Q&A, and — the point of the night — two long, structured networking windows with pre-matched investor introductions in your app.

Bring a business card, bring a one-liner, and bring a specific ask. This is not a mixer.

FAQ

What's the dress code?

Smart cocktail. No suits required. Represent your brand.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Every ticket is single-seat. Buy a second ticket if you'd like a partner or team-mate along — introductions are matched per-attendee.

How do investor intros work?

You'll fill a short "who you'd like to meet" form 5 days before. We match, then push confirmed introductions to the event app on the night.

Refunds?

Full refund up to 7 days before the event, 50% up to 48 hours before, none thereafter. Transferable to another attendee at no charge.

Room fills up fast.
