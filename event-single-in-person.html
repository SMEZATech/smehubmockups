About the Event

The Funding Summit is the single day every year where SA's angel investors, VC partners, DFI teams and grant officers land in one room — with 250 founders they've been asked to meet.

Expect a full day of tight programming: data-backed keynotes, live pitch teardowns, LP panel discussions, and two structured networking windows with pre-matched investor introductions pushed directly to your event app.

Bring a business card, bring a one-liner, and bring a specific ask. This is where capital gets deployed.

Key Topics

State of SA Funding

Exclusive data on exactly where capital is flowing across sectors in 2027.

Pitch Teardowns

Live, brutally honest feedback from active VC partners on three real startup pitches.

LP Perspectives

Alternative Finance

FAQ

What's the dress code?

Smart cocktail. No suits required. Represent your brand.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Every ticket is single-seat. Buy a second ticket if you'd like a partner or team-mate along — introductions are matched per-attendee.

How do investor intros work?

You'll fill a short "who you'd like to meet" form 5 days before. We match, then push confirmed introductions to the event app on the night.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund up to 7 days before the event, 50% up to 48 hours before, none thereafter. Tickets are fully transferable to another attendee at no charge.

Room fills up fast.

Last year sold out 11 days before the doors opened. Don't miss out on securing your seat.
