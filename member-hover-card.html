What it shows, and where it comes from

  • Name, role and business: the profile's position and company (the same line as the feed and "Entrepreneurs to connect with").
  • One line about the business: the first sentence of the bio, two lines at most; hidden when empty.
  • Industry and province chips: from the profile; each hidden when empty.
  • "You both know": up to two names of shared connections; the row is hidden when there are none. Names only, never a count of followers.
  • "Verified business": NOT built yet. Part of the Premium membership later (Joel, 22 Oct). The live card ships without it.
  • Connection's last post: a short quote of their latest post, a reason to start a conversation.
  • Actions: Connect (not connected), Message (connected), Edit my profile (yourself), View profile always.
  • Removed: follower count, follow button, last active, member tag, joined date.
