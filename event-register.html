Registration record (one row per person per event): registration_id, event_id, user_id (member) or guest_email (lowercased) + guest_name, mobile (E.164, optional), status (registered, waitlisted, pending, approved, declined, cancelled, attended, no_show), ticket_type (webinar, general_admission, ...), quantity + seats [{ name, email }], order_id (Woo, paid only), vat_number, method (member or guest), answers [{ question_id, answer }], created_at / updated_at (ISO 8601, +02:00).

Consent ledger, one entry per channel, never pre-ticked: { channel: email_updates | whatsapp | sponsor:<partner_id>, granted: bool, at: timestamp, wording_version: "evt-consent-v1", wording_hash, source_url }. Withdrawal writes a new entry, it never edits the old one. Sponsors: one tick per named sponsor on the event, off by default; export only the people who ticked that sponsor. Confirmation, webinar link, reminders and the replay notice are transactional and need no tick.

Source: source_url, referrer, utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content, plus the first-touch record, and ref (invite link of the person who shared it).
