All three options, as vector SVG and high-resolution PNG. The SVG files are the masters — they carry no fixed size, so they stay perfectly sharp at any scale, from a favicon to vehicle livery or a billboard.

Which file do I use?

SVG
Always, where it is accepted — web, print, signage, anything a designer or printer will touch. Infinitely scalable with no quality loss.
PNG
For PowerPoint, Word, email signatures and tools that will not take SVG. Transparent background.
Reversed / white
Whenever the background is navy, dark or a photograph.
Icon only
Favicons, app icons and social profile pictures, where the full logo would be too small to read.

V1 and V2 are the same cube mark with different colour treatments, so their one-colour white icons are identical files.
