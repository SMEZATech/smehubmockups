Rules for all of them

Email hoursMonday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 SAST. Confirmations, approvals, declines, cancellations, changes, the 1-hour reminder and "we're live" are time-sensitive and go straight away.
SMSOnly to people who gave a number and said yes to event messages. Sent between 08:00 and 20:00 SAST, except time-sensitive ones. Every SMS starts with "SME SA" so people know who it is from, and fits in one 160 character message. A short link service is needed for the links shown here.
WhatsAppSame wording as the SMS for the day-before reminder, the 1-hour reminder, replay and "time changed". Waits for Meta verification.
UnsubscribeInvitations always carry an unsubscribe link. Transactional messages (confirmations, reminders) carry "Cancel my registration".
EditableSubject, wording and send times for the reminders stay editable per event in Mission Control. The others are edited once in Settings.
TestEvery template has a "send me a test", which goes only to the joel+ test inbox.
