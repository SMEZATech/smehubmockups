The State of SA Funding — 2025 Keynote

Dr. Dineo Molefe walks through where the R2.3 billion in reported SME funding actually landed across South Africa in 2025 — by sector, by province, and by deal size — and pulls out the three counter-intuitive shifts founders should pay attention to going into 2026.

The talk closes with a live Q&A on the panel-selected questions: what's changed in DFI mandates, which grant lines are actually paying out, and how much runway a founder needs to survive the current diligence cycle.

You'll take away: a data-backed view of where capital is flowing, three specific mistakes to avoid in your next raise, and the full slide deck to share with your board.

Key takeaways

  • 66% of 2025's committed capital landed in three sectors — fintech, agri and clean energy.
  • Grant lines from IDC and NEF are paying out faster than debt in 2025 — median 71 days vs 118.
  • The typical seed round in SA grew from R2.4m to R3.1m median, but diligence cycles also stretched by ~3 weeks.
  • Township-facing consumer businesses raised 2.1× more than in 2024 — but exits stayed flat.
