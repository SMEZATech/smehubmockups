      Questions

      Who can apply?

      Registered South African companies with a CIPC number. Most of our funding partners need at least a year of trading and a monthly turnover of R50,000 or more.

      Will this affect my credit record?

      A partner you are matched with may do a credit check on you and your business before they decide. We tell you by email and SMS who your application goes to.

      Why do you need my bank statements?

      Funders decide mostly on how money moves through your business account. Six months gives them a fair picture of the good months and the quiet ones, and it means fewer questions later.

      Can I stop and come back?

      Yes. Your answers are saved on this device as you go (never your ID number). Members' answers are also saved to their account.

      I need start-up funding

      Our funding partners fund trading businesses. Our guides on start-up funding cover grants and development finance for new businesses.
