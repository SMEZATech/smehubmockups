Litha Kutta

Head Partnership Development and Ecosystem Coordination · Land Bank

Litha Kutta leads Partnership Development and Ecosystem Coordination at Land Bank, South Africa's development finance institution for agriculture. The role connects agricultural businesses with the partners and finance that help them grow.

The work sits where agriculture, finance and partnership meet, helping farming businesses reach the funders and partners that can grow them.
