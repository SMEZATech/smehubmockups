SME Speaker

Dr. Dineo Molefe

Head of Advisory · Base Capital

Dineo is Head of Advisory at Base Capital, where she helps SME founders turn raw numbers into fundable growth plans. She previously ran analytics at a Johannesburg fintech.

She runs financial modelling workshops for South African founders and holds a doctorate in financial economics from Wits.

Her talks focus on funding strategy and predictive analytics, closing the gap between what DFIs look for and what early-stage founders actually understand.
