About this session

SARS, CIPC and B-BBEE deadlines catch most small businesses out, not because owners do not care but because nobody shows them the calendar. Tax practitioner Marie Roux walks through the dates that matter, what each filing actually involves and how to stay ahead without paying for it twice.

What you will learn

  • The SARS dates most owners miss, and the penalties behind them
  • CIPC annual returns step by step, even if you have not traded
  • When a B-BBEE affidavit is enough and when it is not
  • A simple monthly routine so nothing slips
