Summary

Most small businesses find out about a compliance gap when it costs them: a tender they cannot bid for, a bank that will not open an account, or a penalty from SARS. This session walks through the registrations and returns a South African small business needs, in the order they matter, and shows how to check your own status in an afternoon.

It is for owners who run the business themselves and do not have a company secretary or a full-time accountant.

Key takeaways

  1. 1

    File your CIPC annual return every year, even if the company did not trade. Missing it is how companies get deregistered.

  2. 2

    Keep your tax compliance status green. Funders and corporate buyers check it before anything else.

  3. 3

    Register for UIF and COIDA as soon as you employ one person. Both are required from the first employee.

  4. 4

    File your beneficial ownership record with CIPC and update it when shareholding changes.

  5. 5

    Put the dates in one calendar. A missed date costs more than the hour it takes to set reminders.
