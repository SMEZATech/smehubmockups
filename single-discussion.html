Amahle Jacobs Topic Starter · 14 Aug 2026 at 9:18 am

I'll kick off. My biggest blocker right now is B2B lead generation.

We have a great software product, our current clients love it, and churn is zero. But our sales cycle relies entirely on me tapping my personal network and attending industry events. Cold emailing isn't working for us (open rates are abysmal). Has anyone successfully built an inbound B2B funnel in SA that doesn't just rely on expensive Google Ads?

Tshepho Joel · 14 Aug 2026 at 11:30 am

Hey Amahle, we had this exact problem two years ago. We stopped doing broad cold outreach and switched entirely to Account-Based Marketing (ABM) on LinkedIn.

Instead of emailing 1000 random CEOs, we pick 50 target companies. We connect with multiple stakeholders in each company, comment thoughtfully on their posts for a few weeks, and then send a hyper-personalized voice note or short Loom video highlighting a specific inefficiency we noticed on their site. It takes way more time per lead, but our conversion rate went from 0.5% to 14%.
